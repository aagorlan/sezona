When to start seeds, plant out, feed, cover and harvest — each job in its time.

Weather and warnings

Plot and plantings

Zones, beds and spots, the history of each spot and crop rotation hints.

Season planning

What can still grow on a free bed and when to buy the seeds.

AI assistant

How to join the beta

  1. Join the testers group: groups.google.com/g/sezona-beta
  2. Opt in to the test: play.google.com/apps/testing/app.sezona
  3. Wait 5–10 minutes, then install Sezona from Google Play: play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=app.sezona
    Right after you opt in, Google Play often says the app is unavailable or the file is missing: it takes 5–10 minutes to open the test version for your account. That's normal — wait and open the link again.
  4. Send feedback from the app: More → Support. Ask there for a code — six months of the subscription free.

Questions

How do I find the last frost date for my garden?

Tell Sezona where your plot is. From the last 30 years of weather it works out when your last spring frost and first autumn frost usually are, how long your growing season is and how much warmth the summer brings.

When should I start seeds indoors?

Seed starting is counted back from planting out, and planting out from your plot's last spring frost. Sezona does this for every crop and reminds you when it's time to sow.

Do I need an account?

No. Your plot, plantings and notes are kept on your phone, encrypted. Nothing goes to the server without your consent.

How much does it cost?

The app is free. The full plant catalog on the server and the project's AI come with a subscription; the first 30 days are free. You can also connect the AI helper with your own key.
